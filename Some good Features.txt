### 🎨 GUI & Visual Design Ideas

The "Subtitles" Mode:** A borderless, completely transparent window that floats over your screen, showing translations like live movie subtitles while you take Zoom calls.

Pulsing Voice Orb:** Instead of a flat waveform, make the mic visualizer a glowing circular orb that expands and changes color based on how loud you speak.

The Mini "Pill" Widget:** A tiny, draggable widget (like the iPhone Dynamic Island) that just shows a mic button and the last translated sentence, keeping your screen clutter-free.

Color-Coded Languages:** Automatically tint the app's glow or borders based on the language (e.g., Spanish turns the UI orange, French turns it blue).

Typing Animation:** Instead of text appearing instantly, make the translated text type out letter-by-letter like a sci-fi terminal.

### 🧠 Killer Feature Ideas

gander base output type [like in hindi : ja rahi he(female) , ja raha he(male)]

output in same voice (same to same user voice)

Location-Based Auto-Translate :Skip the language dropdown entirely! A user types or says a location (like "Tokyo" or "France"), and Nova automatically uses a tiny, free Python dictionary (like the countryinfo library) to instantly map the country to its primary spoken language (Japanese or French).

Auto-Detect Language:** Skip the input dropdown. Nova just *knows* what language you are speaking automatically.

Voice-Controlled App:** Let users command the app itself. (*"Nova, clear the screen,"* or *"Nova, switch target to German."*)

Export to Word/TXT:** A one-click button to save your entire translated conversation history as a formatted document.

Custom Wake Words:** A simple text box in settings where the user can rename the assistant to literally anything they want.

Double-Click Dictionary:** Double-click any word in the translated output box to instantly pop up its definition and pronunciation.

TTS Speed Slider:** A UI slider to make the AI voice speak slower (great for learning a language) or faster (for quick reading).

Profanity Filter Toggle:** A "Safe Mode" switch that automatically replaces bad words with asterisks (***) or a *BEEP* sound.

Conversation Stats:** A mini-dashboard showing a word cloud of your most frequently translated words and total translation time.
